Nothing Is Impossible

One climber set out to summit all fourteen of the world's 8,000-metre mountains in a single season, a feat that had taken others most of a decade.

See the peaks

Nirmal "Nims" Purja

Born in Nepal, Nims served in the British Army's Brigade of Gurkhas and later became the first Gurkha to join the UK Special Boat Service. In 2019 he left the military to chase a single goal.

He called it Project Possible: climb every mountain above 8,000 metres, faster than anyone thought a human could, and show the world how far the limits of human endurance really go.

Three Phases

Phase 1 · Nepal

April – May 2019

Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, Kangchenjunga, Everest, Lhotse and Makalu. Six summits, including Everest, Lhotse and Makalu within about 48 hours.

Phase 2 · Pakistan

July 2019

Nanga Parbat, Gasherbrum I, Gasherbrum II, K2 and Broad Peak. Five of the Karakoram's giants, including the notorious K2.

Phase 3 · Tibet & Nepal

September – October 2019

Cho Oyu, Manaslu and finally Shishapangma, which required special permission from China to climb that season.
